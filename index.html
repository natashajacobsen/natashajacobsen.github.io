About Me

From the Bench
to the Boardroom

I started my career as a scientist — spending seven years designing CRISPR libraries, automating high-throughput workflows, and training researchers at Thermo Fisher. One of my products, the Invitrogen™ LentiArray™ CRISPR Library, was named a Top 10 Innovation of 2016 by The Scientist magazine.

That foundation changed how I sell. I don't just pitch instruments — I understand the science behind every workflow decision, the realities of grant cycles, and what it actually takes to build and sustain a research program at scale.

Today, I'm a successful commercial leader specializing in flow cytometry and cell analysis. I manage regional strategy across a multi-million dollar ecosystem spanning capital instrumentation, service, and reagent pull-through — and I build the kind of long-term customer partnerships that outlast product cycles.
